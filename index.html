Red Cell · Blue Team

Two perspectives on the same system

All offensive work described in this portfolio was carried out in isolated, authorised academic lab environments. No live or third-party system was tested.

Red Cell

What an authorised assessment looks for, and how far it is allowed to go.

  • 01Scope and written authorisation before any activity
  • 02Service discovery and surface enumeration
  • 03Vulnerability identification and prioritisation
  • 04Controlled validation — confirm, do not damage
  • 05Findings written as risk, not as a tool dump

Blue Team

The defensive side, which is where most of my academic work sits.

  • 01Know the estate: inventory and exposure baseline
  • 02Harden and assure — including the primitives underneath
  • 03Detect anomalies without disturbing the process
  • 04Investigate with preserved, documented evidence
  • 05Feed every finding back into the controls
